FIRST-TIME SETUP

Allow AirPass Nearby

To discover nearby devices and use gesture handoff, AirPass can ask for camera access for hand tracking and location access for approximate distance sorting. Your camera stays on this device; location sharing is optional and can be turned off.

📷 CameraDetect open-hand and fist gestures. You can stop it any time.
📍 LocationEstimate nearby distance. Coordinates are not shown to other users.
⌁ Nearby discoveryConnect through the AirPass server; not limited to the same Wi-Fi.

Browsers ask permission separately and only after you tap a button. If you deny camera access, manual controls remain available.
